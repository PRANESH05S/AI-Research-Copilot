- They both utilize a combination of machine learning and IoT technologies.

- **AIoT-Based Digital Monitoring Architectures for Water Quality Index Forecasting** primarily discusses digital monitoring architectures using AI and IoT, focusing on distributed learning and federated learning, edge computing, and machine learning. It also highlights the use of various IoT devices for monitoring surface water quality.
- **Critical review on water quality analysis using IoT and machine learning models** focuses on the integration of IoT with GIS-based spatial analysis and machine learning. It emphasizes the importance of combining spatial analysis techniques like geostatistics with machine learning for better water quality assessment.

### Performance Trade-offs

- **Critical review on water quality analysis using IoT and machine learning models** provides detailed insights into the performance of different machine learning models and their effectiveness in assessing WQI. It also evaluates the impact of spatial analysis techniques in GIS on water quality predictions. However, it lacks architectural discussions and specific technical details on the algorithms used.

- **AIoT-Based Digital Monitoring Architectures for Water Quality Index Forecasting** does not mention any limitations.
- **Critical review on water quality analysis using IoT and machine learning models** highlights that data-driven water quality assessment is costly and lacks real-time monitoring, especially for remote areas. It also notes the challenges of incorporating spatial analysis techniques within GIS and the limitation of current WQI models.

### Synthesis & Practical Recommendations
- **Synthesis**: Both papers highlight the importance of integrating IoT with AI and GIS for improving water quality monitoring. They also suggest the use of distributed learning and federated learning for privacy preservation and real-time monitoring. However, they emphasize the need for a robust dataset and the integration of spatial analysis techniques within GIS.
- **Practical Recommendations**:
  - Utilize distributed learning and federated learning for real-time monitoring and privacy preservation.
  - Employ GIS-based spatial analysis techniques like geostatistics for comprehensive water quality assessments.
  - Invest in robust datasets for accurate and reliable WQI predictions.
  - Consider the scalability of solutions to remote and less-accessible areas.
  - Continuous research and development in AI, IoT, and GIS to address the challenges of cost and real-time monitoring.
